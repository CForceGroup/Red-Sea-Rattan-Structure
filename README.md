Red Sea Rattan
Rattan-Inspired Architectural Design at the Red Sea

C.Force Group brings a distinctive architectural character to the Red Sea through a rattan-inspired structure that combines natural visual warmth with modern construction techniques.

Designed to complement the surrounding hospitality environment, the structure creates a welcoming atmosphere while adding a refined architectural element to the destination.

Fiber Resin Mesh & Architectural Design

The project features Fiber Resin Mesh applied over a wooden structure, creating a rattan-inspired appearance while providing a durable and visually appealing architectural finish.

The combination of contemporary materials and natural-inspired design helps create a distinctive experience that blends seamlessly with the surrounding landscape.
